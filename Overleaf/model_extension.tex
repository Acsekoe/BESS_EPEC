\section{Scope and solution concept}

The model is a deterministic capacity-investment EPEC for battery energy
storage systems (BESSs) in a nodal spot market. Each investor chooses nodal
power and energy capacity while anticipating both physical market clearing and
the electricity-price rule. Rival capacities are fixed during an investor's
best response and are updated by diagonalization outside the mathematical
program.

For fixed capacities, the independent system operator (ISO) first minimizes
physical dispatch cost. If this primary market problem admits more than one
valid dual-price vector, the ISO applies a common secondary rule and selects
the valid locational marginal price (LMP) vector with minimum Euclidean norm.
This produces the following hierarchy:
\begin{enumerate}
\item investor $i$ maximizes its daily profit;
\item the primary ISO minimizes physical dispatch cost;
\item over the primary dual-optimal set, the secondary ISO minimizes
      \(\frac12\sum_{n,t}\lambda_{n,t}^{2}\).
\end{enumerate}
The primary ISO problem is represented by primal feasibility, dual feasibility,
and strong duality. The convex secondary problem is represented by its KKT
conditions. The result is one single-level nonconvex MPEC for each investor.

The formulation below contains no quadratic dispatch tie-break, leader-side
price penalty, proximal penalty, relaxed complementarity product, or other
objective regularizer. The minimum-norm objective is a declared ISO price rule,
not an investor penalty and not a perturbation of physical dispatch cost.

\subsection{Decision variables}

Investor $i$'s upper-level variables are
\begin{equation}
x_i=\left\{X^P_{i,n},X^E_{i,n}\right\}_{n\in\mathcal N},
\end{equation}
where $X^P_{i,n}$ and $X^E_{i,n}$ are installed BESS power and energy
capacity. The primary ISO variables are
\begin{equation}
y=\left(P,p^{ch},p^{dis},e,z\right),
\end{equation}
where $P_{g,t}$ is generation, $p^{ch}_{j,n,t}$ and
$p^{dis}_{j,n,t}$ are cleared BESS charging and discharging power,
$e_{j,n,\tau}$ is stored energy, and $z_{n,t}$ is nodal net injection.

The primary dual variables are collected in
\begin{equation}\label{eq:primary-dual-vector}
u=\left(\lambda,\lambda^{sys},\nu,\mu^{up},\mu^{dn},
\rho,\sigma,\gamma,\delta,\rho^{per}\right).
\end{equation}
The LMP is $\lambda_{n,t}$, while $\lambda_t^{sys}$ is the system-balance
multiplier. The remaining dual variables are defined with their constraints
below.

\section{Investor problem}

For fixed rival capacities $X_{-i}$, investor $i$'s feasible investment set
is
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:investment-feasibility}
\begin{align}
0\le X^P_{i,n}
&\le \overline X_n-\sum_{j\ne i}X^P_{j,n},
&&\forall n,\label{eq:headroom}\\
r^{\min}X^P_{i,n}\le X^E_{i,n}
&\le r^{\max}X^P_{i,n},
&&\forall n.\label{eq:duration}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
The capital-recovery factor and daily investment cost are
\begin{align}
CRF_i&=\frac{w_i(1+w_i)^{L_i}}{(1+w_i)^{L_i}-1},\label{eq:crf}\\
K_i&=\frac{CRF_i}{365.25}\sum_n
\left(c_i^P X^P_{i,n}+c_i^E X^E_{i,n}\right).\label{eq:daily-capex}
\end{align}
Daily investor profit is
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:profit}
\begin{align}
\Pi_i={}&R_i^{BESS}+R_i^{gen}-C_i^{deg}-K_i,\label{eq:profit-total}\\
R_i^{BESS}={}&\sum_{n,t}\lambda_{n,t}
\left(p^{dis}_{i,n,t}-p^{ch}_{i,n,t}\right),\label{eq:bess-revenue}\\
R_i^{gen}={}&\sum_{g,t}s_{i,g}
\left(\lambda_{n(g),t}-C_g\right)P_{g,t},\label{eq:generator-rent}\\
C_i^{deg}={}&\frac{d_i}{2}\sum_{n,t}
\left(p^{ch}_{i,n,t}+p^{dis}_{i,n,t}\right).\label{eq:private-degradation}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
Stand-alone merchant investors have $s_{i,g}=0$. Portfolio investors
internalize the spot rents of their owned generation shares.

\section{Primary ISO: cost-minimizing dispatch}

For fixed capacities $X=(X_i,X_{-i})$, the ISO solves the linear program
\begin{equation}\label{eq:primary-iso}
\min_y\quad
Z^P(y)=\sum_{g,t}C_gP_{g,t}
+\frac12\sum_{j,n,t}d_j
\left(p^{ch}_{j,n,t}+p^{dis}_{j,n,t}\right)
\end{equation}
subject to
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:primary-primal}
\begin{align}
z_{n,t}
&=\sum_{g\in\mathcal G_n}P_{g,t}
+\sum_j\left(p^{dis}_{j,n,t}-p^{ch}_{j,n,t}\right)-D_{n,t},
&&\forall n,t,\label{eq:nodal-balance}\\
\sum_nz_{n,t}&=0,
&&\forall t,\label{eq:system-balance}\\
0\le P_{g,t}&\le\overline P_{g,t},
&&\forall g,t,\label{eq:generation-capacity}\\
-\overline F_l\le\sum_nH_{l,n}z_{n,t}&\le\overline F_l,
&&\forall l,t,\label{eq:transmission-capacity}\\
0\le p^{ch}_{j,n,t}&\le X^P_{j,n},
&&\forall j,n,t,\label{eq:charge-capacity}\\
0\le p^{dis}_{j,n,t}&\le X^P_{j,n},
&&\forall j,n,t,\label{eq:discharge-capacity}\\
e_{j,n,t}&=e_{j,n,t-1}+\eta p^{ch}_{j,n,t}
-\eta^{-1}p^{dis}_{j,n,t},
&&\forall j,n,t,\label{eq:soc-transition}\\
0\le e_{j,n,\tau}&\le X^E_{j,n},
&&\forall j,n,\tau,\label{eq:soc-capacity}\\
e_{j,n,0}&=e_{j,n,T},
&&\forall j,n.\label{eq:soc-periodicity}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
There is no fixed initial state of charge; only daily periodicity is imposed.

\subsection{Dual feasibility}

Because $P,p^{ch},p^{dis},e$ are nonnegative, their stationarity conditions
are inequalities. Because $z$ is free, its stationarity condition is an
equality:
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:primary-dual-feasibility}
\begin{align}
\lambda_{n(g),t}+\nu_{g,t}
&\le C_g,
&&\forall g,t,\label{eq:dual-generation}\\
-\lambda_{n,t}+\rho_{j,n,t}-\eta\gamma_{j,n,t}
&\le \frac{d_j}{2},
&&\forall j,n,t,\label{eq:dual-charge}\\
\lambda_{n,t}+\sigma_{j,n,t}+\eta^{-1}\gamma_{j,n,t}
&\le \frac{d_j}{2},
&&\forall j,n,t,\label{eq:dual-discharge}\\
-\lambda_{n,t}+\lambda_t^{sys}
+\sum_lH_{l,n}\left(\mu^{up}_{l,t}+\mu^{dn}_{l,t}\right)
&=0,
&&\forall n,t.\label{eq:dual-injection}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
Define the SOC reduced-cost expression
\begin{align}
A^E_{j,n,\tau}:={}&\delta_{j,n,\tau}
+\mathbf 1_{\{\tau\in\mathcal T\}}\gamma_{j,n,\tau}
-\mathbf 1_{\{\tau+1\in\mathcal T\}}\gamma_{j,n,\tau+1}
\notag\\
&+\mathbf 1_{\{\tau=0\}}\rho^{per}_{j,n}
-\mathbf 1_{\{\tau=T\}}\rho^{per}_{j,n}.
\label{eq:soc-reduced-cost}
\end{align}
SOC dual feasibility is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dual-soc}
A^E_{j,n,\tau}\le0,
\qquad\forall j,n,\tau.
\end{equation}

For use in the secondary price-selection problem, define the nonnegative
slacks
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:dual-slacks}
\begin{align}
s^G_{g,t}&:=C_g-\lambda_{n(g),t}-\nu_{g,t},\label{eq:slack-generation}\\
s^{ch}_{j,n,t}&:=\frac{d_j}{2}+\lambda_{n,t}
-\rho_{j,n,t}+\eta\gamma_{j,n,t},\label{eq:slack-charge}\\
s^{dis}_{j,n,t}&:=\frac{d_j}{2}-\lambda_{n,t}
-\sigma_{j,n,t}-\eta^{-1}\gamma_{j,n,t},\label{eq:slack-discharge}\\
s^E_{j,n,\tau}&:=-A^E_{j,n,\tau}.\label{eq:slack-soc}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
Thus $s^G,s^{ch},s^{dis},s^E\ge0$. Define also the equality residual
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dual-equality-residual}
r^z_{n,t}:=-\lambda_{n,t}+\lambda_t^{sys}
+\sum_lH_{l,n}\left(\mu^{up}_{l,t}+\mu^{dn}_{l,t}\right)=0.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Strong duality}

The primary dual objective is
\begin{align}
Z^D(u;X)={}&
\sum_{n,t}D_{n,t}\lambda_{n,t}
+\sum_{g,t}\overline P_{g,t}\nu_{g,t}
+\sum_{l,t}\overline F_l
\left(\mu^{up}_{l,t}-\mu^{dn}_{l,t}\right)
\notag\\
&+\sum_{j,n,t}X^P_{j,n}
\left(\rho_{j,n,t}+\sigma_{j,n,t}\right)
+\sum_{j,n,\tau}X^E_{j,n}\delta_{j,n,\tau}.
\label{eq:primary-dual-objective}
\end{align}
Define the primary primal--dual gap
\begin{equation}\label{eq:primary-gap}
q(y,u;X):=Z^P(y)-Z^D(u;X).
\end{equation}
Primary primal feasibility, primary dual feasibility, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:primary-strong-duality}
q(y,u;X)=0
\end{equation}
are necessary and sufficient for an optimum of the primary ISO linear program.
No explicit primary complementarity products are needed.

\section{Secondary ISO: minimum-norm LMP selection}

For a primary-optimal dispatch $y$ and fixed capacities $X$, define the
primary dual-optimal face by the dual slacks, equality residuals, strong
duality, and declared dual-variable domains. The secondary ISO solves
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:secondary-iso}
\begin{align}
\min_u\quad &\frac12\sum_{n,t}\lambda_{n,t}^2,
\label{eq:secondary-objective}\\
\text{s.t.}\quad
&s^G,s^{ch},s^{dis},s^E\ge0,\label{eq:secondary-dual-ineq}\\
&r^z=0,\qquad q=0,\label{eq:secondary-equalities}\\
&\underline u_m\le u_m\le\overline u_m,
&&\forall m.\label{eq:secondary-bounds}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
The bounds in \eqref{eq:secondary-bounds} include the natural sign domains of
the primary multipliers. Finite electricity-price domains may also be declared
as part of the market specification. They are constraints, not objective
regularizers. If a theoretical dual variable is unbounded on one side, the
corresponding bound and its KKT multiplier below are omitted.

The feasible set of \eqref{eq:secondary-iso} is convex and its objective is
strictly convex in $\lambda$. Consequently, the selected LMP vector is unique,
although non-price primary multipliers can remain nonunique.

\section{KKT system of the secondary ISO}

Associate nonnegative multipliers
\begin{equation}
\alpha^G,\alpha^{ch},\alpha^{dis},\alpha^E\ge0
\end{equation}
with the four groups of nonnegative dual-feasibility slacks. Associate free
multipliers $\beta_{n,t}$ and $\theta$ with $r^z_{n,t}=0$ and $q=0$,
respectively. Finally, associate nonnegative multipliers
\(\xi_m^-\) and \(\xi_m^+\) with the lower and upper bounds of $u_m$.

The secondary Lagrangian is
\begin{align}
\mathcal L^{MN}={}&
\frac12\sum_{n,t}\lambda_{n,t}^2
-\sum_{g,t}\alpha^G_{g,t}s^G_{g,t}
-\sum_{j,n,t}\alpha^{ch}_{j,n,t}s^{ch}_{j,n,t}
\notag\\
&-\sum_{j,n,t}\alpha^{dis}_{j,n,t}s^{dis}_{j,n,t}
-\sum_{j,n,\tau}\alpha^E_{j,n,\tau}s^E_{j,n,\tau}
+\sum_{n,t}\beta_{n,t}r^z_{n,t}
+\theta q
\notag\\
&-\sum_m\xi_m^-(u_m-\underline u_m)
-\sum_m\xi_m^+(\overline u_m-u_m).
\label{eq:secondary-lagrangian}
\end{align}

\subsection{Secondary stationarity}

Stationarity with respect to every component of the primary dual vector
\eqref{eq:primary-dual-vector} gives
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:secondary-stationarity}
\begin{align}
0={}&\lambda_{n,t}
+\sum_{g\in\mathcal G_n}\alpha^G_{g,t}
-\sum_j\alpha^{ch}_{j,n,t}
+\sum_j\alpha^{dis}_{j,n,t}
-\beta_{n,t}-\theta D_{n,t}
-\xi^-_{\lambda_{n,t}}+\xi^+_{\lambda_{n,t}},
&&\forall n,t,\label{eq:mn-stat-lambda}\\
0={}&\sum_n\beta_{n,t}
-\xi^-_{\lambda_t^{sys}}+\xi^+_{\lambda_t^{sys}},
&&\forall t,\label{eq:mn-stat-system-lambda}\\
0={}&\alpha^G_{g,t}-\theta\overline P_{g,t}
-\xi^-_{\nu_{g,t}}+\xi^+_{\nu_{g,t}},
&&\forall g,t,\label{eq:mn-stat-nu}\\
0={}&\sum_nH_{l,n}\beta_{n,t}-\theta\overline F_l
-\xi^-_{\mu^{up}_{l,t}}+\xi^+_{\mu^{up}_{l,t}},
&&\forall l,t,\label{eq:mn-stat-mu-up}\\
0={}&\sum_nH_{l,n}\beta_{n,t}+\theta\overline F_l
-\xi^-_{\mu^{dn}_{l,t}}+\xi^+_{\mu^{dn}_{l,t}},
&&\forall l,t,\label{eq:mn-stat-mu-dn}\\
0={}&\alpha^{ch}_{j,n,t}-\theta X^P_{j,n}
-\xi^-_{\rho_{j,n,t}}+\xi^+_{\rho_{j,n,t}},
&&\forall j,n,t,\label{eq:mn-stat-rho}\\
0={}&\alpha^{dis}_{j,n,t}-\theta X^P_{j,n}
-\xi^-_{\sigma_{j,n,t}}+\xi^+_{\sigma_{j,n,t}},
&&\forall j,n,t,\label{eq:mn-stat-sigma}\\
0={}&-\eta\alpha^{ch}_{j,n,t}
+\eta^{-1}\alpha^{dis}_{j,n,t}
+\alpha^E_{j,n,t}-\alpha^E_{j,n,t-1}
-\xi^-_{\gamma_{j,n,t}}+\xi^+_{\gamma_{j,n,t}},
&&\forall j,n,t,\label{eq:mn-stat-gamma}\\
0={}&\alpha^E_{j,n,\tau}-\theta X^E_{j,n}
-\xi^-_{\delta_{j,n,\tau}}+\xi^+_{\delta_{j,n,\tau}},
&&\forall j,n,\tau,\label{eq:mn-stat-delta}\\
0={}&\alpha^E_{j,n,0}-\alpha^E_{j,n,T}
-\xi^-_{\rho^{per}_{j,n}}+\xi^+_{\rho^{per}_{j,n}},
&&\forall j,n.\label{eq:mn-stat-periodicity}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}

\subsection{Secondary primal feasibility, dual feasibility, and complementarity}

The remaining KKT conditions are
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:secondary-kkt-rest}
\begin{align}
&s^G,s^{ch},s^{dis},s^E\ge0,
\qquad r^z=0,
\qquad q=0,
\qquad \underline u\le u\le\overline u,
\label{eq:mn-primal-feasibility}\\
&\alpha^G,\alpha^{ch},\alpha^{dis},\alpha^E,\xi^-,\xi^+\ge0,
\qquad \beta,\theta\text{ free},
\label{eq:mn-dual-feasibility}\\
&\alpha^G_{g,t}s^G_{g,t}=0,
&&\forall g,t,\label{eq:mn-comp-generation}\\
&\alpha^{ch}_{j,n,t}s^{ch}_{j,n,t}=0,
\qquad
\alpha^{dis}_{j,n,t}s^{dis}_{j,n,t}=0,
&&\forall j,n,t,\label{eq:mn-comp-storage-power}\\
&\alpha^E_{j,n,\tau}s^E_{j,n,\tau}=0,
&&\forall j,n,\tau,\label{eq:mn-comp-soc}\\
&\xi^-_m(u_m-\underline u_m)=0,
\qquad
\xi^+_m(\overline u_m-u_m)=0,
&&\forall m.\label{eq:mn-comp-bounds}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
In the implementation, the nonnegative products in
\eqref{eq:mn-comp-generation}--\eqref{eq:mn-comp-bounds} are summed and their
sum is constrained to zero. This aggregate equality is mathematically
equivalent to imposing every product separately.

\section{Complete single-level investor MPEC}

Let
\begin{equation}
v=\left(\alpha^G,\alpha^{ch},\alpha^{dis},\alpha^E,
\beta,\theta,\xi^-,\xi^+\right)
\end{equation}
collect the secondary KKT multipliers. Investor $i$'s complete best-response
MPEC is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:complete-mpec}
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\max_{X_i^P,X_i^E,y,u,v}\quad &\Pi_i\\
\text{s.t.}\quad
&\eqref{eq:investment-feasibility},\\
&\eqref{eq:primary-primal},\\
&\eqref{eq:dual-slacks},\ \eqref{eq:dual-equality-residual},\\
  \eqref{eq:primary-strong-duality},\\
  \underline u\le u\le\overline u,\\
&\eqref{eq:secondary-stationarity},\\
&\eqref{eq:secondary-kkt-rest},
\end{aligned}}
\qquad\text{given }X_{-i}.
\end{equation}
The primary primal variables $y$, primary dual variables $u$, and secondary
KKT multipliers $v$ are endogenous inside the investor problem. Products of
investment variables and primary duals in \eqref{eq:primary-dual-objective},
LMP settlement in \eqref{eq:profit}, and the secondary complementarity
conditions make \eqref{eq:complete-mpec} nonconvex even though both ISO
problems are convex when the upper-level capacities and primary dispatch are
fixed.

Crucially, the investor does not minimize the price norm and cannot trade a
larger norm against higher profit. Equations
\eqref{eq:secondary-stationarity}--\eqref{eq:secondary-kkt-rest} require the
ISO's minimum-norm problem to be optimal before $\Pi_i$ is evaluated. The
investor can change prices economically by changing capacity, but it cannot
select a personally favourable LMP vector from a fixed primary dual-optimal
face.

\section{EPEC equilibrium and diagonalization}

A generalized Nash equilibrium is a jointly feasible capacity profile
$X^*$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:epec-fixed-point}
X_i^*\in BR_i(X_{-i}^*)
:=\arg\max\left\{\Pi_i:
\eqref{eq:complete-mpec}\text{ holds for }X_{-i}=X_{-i}^*\right\},
\qquad\forall i\in\mathcal I.
\end{equation}
The implementation searches for this fixed point by Gauss--Jacobi or
Gauss--Seidel diagonalization. Under Jacobi updating, every investor solves
against one common frozen rival snapshot:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:jacobi-update}
X_i^{k+1}=(1-\omega)X_i^k+\omega\widehat X_i^k,
\qquad
\widehat X_i^k\in BR_i(X_{-i}^k),
\qquad 0<\omega\le1.
\end{equation}
Damping $\omega$ belongs to the numerical fixed-point algorithm and does not
alter the best-response objective or the equilibrium definition
\eqref{eq:epec-fixed-point}. A run is an EPEC result only if every investor
best response is solved successfully and the undamped fixed-point residuals
converge.

After convergence, all final capacities are fixed and the ISO is independently
re-cleared using the same primary dispatch and minimum-norm price rule. At a
valid fixed point, the embedded LMP vector in every investor MPEC must agree,
up to solver tolerance, with this common joint-settlement LMP vector.
