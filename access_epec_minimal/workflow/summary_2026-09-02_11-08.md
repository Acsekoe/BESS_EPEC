# Session summary: strategic renewable and storage bidding, Phases 1-3

Implements the plan in `workflow/summary.md` (strategic generation offers on
an owner-split renewable fleet) with one user-directed change made mid-session:
**the truthful PV offer is 0 EUR/MWh like wind (true cost 0, no -25 default),
and strategic offers may lie above or below truthful** (two-sided bounds of
+/-500 EUR/MWh instead of the plan's markup-only floor). The -25 EUR/MWh
support configuration remains available via `--pv-true-cost -25`.

## Decisions

- `generation_cost` keeps the true-economic-cost role; a new optional
  `generation_offer` mapping (default: equal to cost) carries the submitted
  ISO offer. All ISO-side uses (LP objective, dual stationarity, KKT products,
  warm-start objectives) now read the effective offer; all profit/rent terms
  keep true cost. With no offer field present every historical input and run
  is bit-compatible.
- New formulation modules `mpec_strategic_portfolio_relaxed_kkt.py` and
  `run_portfolio_bid_game.py` are added as the plan explicitly mandates
  (recorded decision; the maintained storage-price game is untouched).
- Offer variables exist only for owned (generator, hour) pairs with positive
  availability (52 per portfolio investor: 24 wind + 2x14 PV daylight hours).
- Strategic generation requires 100% unit ownership (owner-split input);
  fractional aggregate shares are rejected.
- Phase-2/3 fixed capacities use
  `output/three_investors_without_i2_bid_penalty_0p001_20_sweeps/final_capacities.csv`
  as the common reference profile (the maintained I1/I3/I4 run).
- Every portfolio run ends with an exact reclear plus truthful-offer
  counterfactuals; MPEC-claimed profit is never reported without the
  recleared value next to it.

   gen-offer proximal 0.01, storage-bid proximal 0.001, eps 1e-3)

fixed truthful (+/-7.5) unless strategic. All profits below are EXACT
RECLEARED EUR/day (true costs), not MPEC claims.

|------------------------------------------|--------|---------|---------|

### Zero-proximal audit of run A (repo rule)

At the final state with proximal_generation_penalty = 0: I1 deviation 0;
I3 moves offers by up to 334 EUR/MWh for +644 EUR/day exact gain (+0.5%);
I4 moves 164 EUR/MWh for +349 EUR/day (+0.2%). The profit surface is a
plateau in offer space: offer LEVELS are weakly identified, offer EFFECTS
(prices, dispatch, profits) are robust. Run A is an approximate equilibrium
with unilateral exact-reclear gains <= ~650 EUR/day.

### Mechanism (run A, exact reclear vs truthful reclear)

- I4 (80% PV) lifts midday offers at N6 h12-16 to ~8 EUR/MWh (h14: 38);
  I3 (20% PV) lifts only ~2 EUR/MWh -- ownership-share asymmetry. Wind
  offers stay truthful (no local market power at N1 beside G_IEEE1).
- Midday LMPs at PV nodes rise 30.07 -> 38.45 (+8.4); total renewable
  curtailment changes +0.3 MWh on 12,930 MWh. This is price support through
  the marginal curtailed unit setting the price, NOT quantity withholding.
- The same valley lift compresses storage arbitrage: every investor's
  storage spot revenue falls ~4,400 -> ~2,800 EUR/day; standalone storage
  P&L (spot - degradation - capex) is negative for ALL investors at the
  reference fleet. I3/I4 don't care (gen rents dominate); merchant I1 turns
  loss-making (-1,174) under truthful storage prices and defends only to
  ~break-even (67) when its bids are strategic (run C).
- truthful_I3 counterfactual in A changes nothing (deltas ~0): I3's +3,375
  vs truthful is pure free-riding on I4's price support.
- Storage-only case (B): strategic storage bidding transfers FROM renewable
  owners (I4 -39,413 vs truthful; I3 -4,921) with I1 gaining +5,009 --
  strategic charging patterns keep midday PV-node prices depressed.

### Support-control sensitivity (run D, `portfolio_gen_only_pv_minus25_40_sweeps`)

Gen-only game with PV true cost -25 EUR/MWh (`--pv-true-cost -25`), all else
as run A. Result pattern identical in kind: exact recleared I4 273,338 vs
260,850 truthful (+12,491), I3 free-rides +2,913 (truthful_I3 deltas ~0),
I1 370 -> -671. Strategic gain smaller than the 0-cost world (+12.5k vs
+18.0k for I4). NOT converged by the raw rule: gen-offer residual settles
into a ~2.0-2.2 EUR/MWh limit cycle (plateau oscillation, tol 0.5); six
isolated transient I3 worker crashes.

### Phase 4: full portfolio EPEC (endogenous capacity, both strategic)

Fresh symmetric start (5 MW / 15 MWh), damping 0.15, 40 sweeps
(`portfolio_full_epec_damp015_40_sweeps`) + 30-sweep continuation
(`portfolio_full_epec_damp015_sweeps_41_70`).

- NOT converged at sweep 40 (power residual 5.1 MW, contracting ~0.85/sweep
  with occasional best-response jumps, e.g. +13 MW at sweep 34); still
  contracting at sweep ~50 (3 MW).
- Interim capacity totals (sweep 40): I4 79.3 > I1 70.2 > I3 57.6 MW,
  spread 21.8 MW (storage-only baseline: ~9.7 MW). All investors
  concentrate at PV nodes N6/N8.
- **Generation offers stayed exactly truthful for the entire run — and the
  zero-proximal multistart audit shows this is a warm-start ARTIFACT, not
  an equilibrium feature.** At the sweep-40 state with capacities/bids
  frozen: I3 has a +2,282 EUR/day and I4 a +10,851 EUR/day exact-recleared
  offer deviation (robust across truthful-start and runA-seeded
  initializations). Mechanism: an inframarginal unit's offer has zero
  gradient at truthful 0 until it reaches the local LMP (~30 EUR/MWh), so
  warm-started joint BRs improve through the capacity/bid subspace and
  never cross the plateau. Warm-started Jacobi under-explores the offer
  dimension from truthful starts in the joint game.
- The truthful-offer trajectory is therefore reinterpreted as the matrix
  CONTROL case (endogenous capacity + strategic storage, generation de
  facto truthful). The genuine full EPEC is restarted from an offer-seeded
  state (see below).

### Full EPEC with active offer channel (offer-seeded restart)

Because unaided warm-started Jacobi never leaves truthful offers (see audit
above), the state at sweep 70 was re-seeded: I3/I4 zero-proximal offer BRs
re-solved at frozen capacities/bids and written into
`portfolio_full_epec_offer_seeded_state/final_generation_offers.csv`, then
resumed 40 sweeps (`portfolio_full_epec_offer_seeded_40_sweeps`, sweeps
71-110). The seed held: gen-offer residual stays ~1e-3 (BRs re-propose the
manipulated profile; snap-back would read ~330).

- Capacity totals (sweep 110): **I4 107.2 > I3 43.8 > I1 41.1 MW, spread
  66.1 MW** (truthful-offer control branch: 23.6; storage-only baseline:
  9.7). Energy: I4 321.7 vs I3 124.8 vs I1 101.2 MWh. Strategic generation
  offers therefore produce a strong capacity separation: the PV-heavy
  investor roughly doubles its fleet while the merchant shrinks.
- NOT converged: power residual 1.69 MW (tol 0.5) and the storage-bid
  residual still oscillates 2.4-10.3 EUR/MWh (persistent joint-game mode);
  gen offers stable. Ordering and direction are clear; exact levels are not
  final. One transient failure (sweep 106).
- Exact-recleared profits at final state: I1 66, I3 124,265, I4 177,455.
  Claimed-minus-recleared: I4 +14,371 (non-fixed-point, oscillating bids).
- Counterfactuals (capacities/bids frozen): reverting ONE investor's offers
  to truthful barely matters (I4 -460, I3 -240) but reverting BOTH costs
  I4 -54,974 and I3 -12,444 while I1 gains +3,080/day: each investor's
  curtailed marginal unit alone suffices to support the midday price --
  mutually redundant price support, not free-riding, at these capacities.

## Next steps

- Convergence: the joint game's storage-bid residual oscillates (2-10
  EUR/MWh) and capacities show recurring BR jumps. Candidate remedies:
  lower damping (0.10), higher bid proximal, or branch-aware BRs
  (multistart inside the loop) as in the old access-game diagnosis.
- Consider a permanent `--seed-offers` option in the runner: truthful
  warm starts provably trap the offer channel (zero gradient below the
  LMP), so seeding is methodologically required for full-EPEC runs -- an
  explicit decision to record before changing the maintained runner.
- Robustness: `--include-i2`; separate-inverter sensitivity; generation
  proximal 0.001; re-audit any equilibrium candidate with all proximal
  penalties zero (repo rule) -- run A passed this in the value sense
  (unilateral exact gains <= ~650 EUR/day) but offer levels sit on a
  plateau; the seeded full-EPEC state has NOT yet had a zero-proximal
  capacity+bid+offer audit.
- Interpretation for the thesis: report offer EFFECTS (LMP lift, spread
  compression, capacity separation, profit deltas vs truthful), never
  offer levels; always exact-recleared numbers.
