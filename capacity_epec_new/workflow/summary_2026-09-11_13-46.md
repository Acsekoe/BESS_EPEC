# Jacobi capacity totals are stable, but the final Nash audit fails

**Written:** 2026-09-11 13:46 CEST  
**Project:** `BESS_EPEC/capacity_epec_new`  
**Run:** `init5mw15mwh_jacobi_rho100_d025_s100`  
**Configuration:** capacity-only relaxed-KKT, energy-only market, Jacobi updates, damping `0.25`, `100` sweeps, regret tolerance `0.001`

## Outcome

The run stopped after the maximum 100 sweeps and did **not** converge. Investor-level portfolio totals were comparatively stable over the final ten sweeps, but the allocation of those capacities across nodes continued to move. More importantly, the valid final zero-proximal audit found profitable unilateral deviations for all three investors. The final candidate therefore fails the Nash criterion.

Nodal instability by itself would not prove that a Nash equilibrium is absent: investors could move among equivalent nodal allocations without gaining profit. Here, the rejection comes from the positive profit improvements after market reclearing. The nodal movements are the mechanism and a visible symptom, not the formal Nash test.

## Final Nash audit

| Investor | Current profit (EUR/day) | Recleared deviation profit (EUR/day) | Profitable deviation / regret (EUR/day) | Relative regret | Maximum nodal deviation (MW / MWh) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| I1 | 1,920.92 | 3,572.68 | 1,651.76 | 86.0% | 0.197 / 0.350 |
| I2 | 73,564.63 | 75,764.69 | 2,200.07 | 3.0% | 7.033 / 31.736 |
| I3 | 90,099.79 | 125,941.73 | 35,841.94 | 39.8% | 16.439 / 105.934 |

The configured relative-regret tolerance is `0.001`, or **0.1%**. I1 has the largest relative incentive to deviate, while I3 has the largest absolute incentive and the largest capacity deviation. `stable_sweeps` remained zero.

The audit is marked valid, all three deviation solves terminated optimally, and the reported complementarity violation is zero. The run does not provide a global best-response certificate, but that does not weaken the rejection: one valid profitable unilateral deviation is sufficient to show that the reported strategy profile is not a Nash equilibrium. Global certification would be needed to certify equilibrium when no profitable deviation is found.

## Interpretation

- Investor aggregate capacities appear close to a stable portfolio scale.
- The full strategic variables are nodal power and energy capacities, and those allocations are not stable.
- Similar aggregate totals can still produce different congestion, prices, dispatch, and investor profits when capacity moves between nodes.
- The formal reason the Nash criterion is not fulfilled is the positive recleared regret, especially I3's EUR 35,841.94/day deviation gain and I1's 86.0% relative regret.
- Because `multistart_every_sweeps = 0`, no ordinary sweep could certify a regret-based stop. The final zero-proximal audit is the authoritative check, and it failed.
