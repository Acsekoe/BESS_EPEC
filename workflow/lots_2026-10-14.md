# Hosting rights in lots at N5: payoff table

2026-10-14 18:46 (Europe/Vienna). Commit base `b0b74b8` plus uncommitted
`--allocation` (`own_limit` in `prepare_input.py`, `own_limits` in
`capacity_clearing.py`). 5-bus system, flexible demand 10 / 25 MW, eps 1e-4,
investors I1, I3, I4 (I2 dropped). Python 3.13.7, Pyomo 6.10.1, Gurobi 13.0.3,
Ipopt 3.13.2. Data sha256 `33b99c1b…` (in each `*_run_config.json`).

N5 hosting capacity 100 MW, split into two 50 MW lots. One run per assignment:
Gauss-Seidel best responses (relaxed MPEC, Ipopt, local) in the order I3 I4 I1,
everyone free at the other nodes, each investor within its right at N5. All
seven runs converged (a round without moves; local, not proven). Results in
`lots_2026-10-14/` (capacities, summaries, run configs, `lot_table.json`,
`welfare_compare.json`).

## Findings

- **Social cost follows the node's total MWh**, not the holder: 417-431 MWh
  +1 479 to +1 574, 446 MWh +1 761, 477 MWh +2 194 / +2 196. I4 holding all or
  half gives the same 476.7 MWh, the same social cost and the same profit sum.
- **WTP for the whole right** (vs. the rival that would hold it instead): I1
  5 958, I3 5 970, I4 23 399-23 451, i.e. about 60, 60 and 234 EUR/MW/day.
  Against the node staying closed: 5 958, 7 775, 30 839.
- **I4 sizes for the price, not the arbitrage.** As holder it builds 4.8 h
  (477 MWh) against 4.2 h for I1 or I3; the right caps MW only. The highest
  bidder is the worst holder (about +650/day against I3 or I1).
- **50 MW lots:** I4's first lot is worth 12 160 (other lot with I1) to 19 966
  (other lot with I3; vs. I1 holding it). Its second lot is worth -1 681 if I3
  would hold it and 11 291 if I1 would. I3 and I1 value one lot at 2 251-3 744
  and 2 390-2 404. I3 prefers I4 to hold N5 (116 899 vs 116 026 holding all
  itself): free-riding on I4's price lift.
- **The best filled allocation (I1 50, I3 50) is still +1 479**, almost all
  from N2 (71.6 / 432 against the planner's 63 / 359), which no N5 rule touches.

## Limitations

Local equilibria from one order and start; Gurobi `verify --allocation` not run.
Differences below a few hundred EUR/day are uncertain. One day, stylized
flexible demand. Payments are not modelled in the runs; auction outcomes are
derived from the table.
