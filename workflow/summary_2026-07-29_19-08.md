## Implementation completed

### Direct epsilon-times-square strategy penalty

Added CLI option `--strategic-epsilon-penalty`. The upper-level objective now
subtracts the literal, unnormalized term

`epsilon * sum((X_power - Q_charge)**2 + (X_power - Q_discharge)**2 + p_charge**2 + p_discharge**2)`.

The price variables are pinned toward zero. Quantity is represented as withheld
quantity `X_power - Q` so the simple square selects full availability; directly
penalizing `Q**2` would incorrectly reward offering zero capacity. The penalty
is exported separately from the existing proximal penalty. A coefficient of
`1e-6` would have charged approximately EUR 8/day to I3 and EUR 19/day to I4 at
the old final bids, so it is intended as a small equilibrium selector.

### Absolute convergence and three-sweep confirmation

The strategic-operation stopping rule no longer uses maximum-relative changes.
Those values remain exported as diagnostics. Convergence now requires every
investor to satisfy all three absolute metrics:

- capacity step norm below 0.5 MW-equivalent, using MW and MWh/4;
- withheld-offer step norm below 0.25 MW;
- bid-price step norm below 0.5 EUR/MWh;
- all conditions must hold for three consecutive complete sweeps.

The streak is reset after any failed solve or failed metric, printed each sweep,
written to `iteration_history.csv`, and persisted in `checkpoint.json`.
