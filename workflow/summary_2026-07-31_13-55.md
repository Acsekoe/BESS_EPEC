# Gauss--Jacobi EPEC findings and strategic-operation direction

## Current objective

Use symmetric Gauss--Jacobi diagonalization as the preferred EPEC solution convention, then extend it to the strategic-operation model with investment, hourly quantity offers, and two-sided bid prices. The strategic model still requires experiments with regularization and convergence diagnostics. Because its four best responses are expensive, full Jacobi should support independent parallel worker processes.

## Capacity-only results

- The original IEEE-9 distributed-congestion data were run with four investors, full Gauss--Jacobi updates, damping 0.25, a 200 MW nodal limit, the calibrated conventional generators, and the N5 peaker.
- The run converged in 21 iterations with no projection events: `model/output/jacobi_old_data_2026-07-31/damping025/order_I1-I2-I3-I4/`.
- Exported totals are 177.319 MW / 594.808 MWh. About 0.72 MW is decaying uniform-seed residue across economically inactive nodes. The meaningful N6/N8 fleet is 176.596 MW / 593.363 MWh.
- Siting is 166.218 MW at N8 and 10.378 MW at N6. This essentially reproduces the comparable Seidel result of 176.802 MW / 594.041 MWh, with 166.253 MW at N8 and 10.544 MW at N6.
- The 200 MW N8 limit is slack and access multipliers are effectively zero. The old N3/N9 relocation at a 100 MW nodal limit was therefore mainly overflow from a binding N8 limit.
- Jacobi ownership is relatively balanced: I1 39.181 MW, I2 38.954 MW, I3 46.063 MW, and I4 53.121 MW. Comparable Seidel orders allocate ownership very differently even when aggregate investment is similar. This supports robust aggregate investment/siting but algorithm-selected ownership.
- Joint price and rent selection remain non-robust. Jacobi joint LMPs span 19.16--84.55 EUR/MWh, whereas a nearly identical Seidel physical solution spans about 0.04--74.59 EUR/MWh. Optimistic MPEC profits also differ materially from joint-settlement profits.

## Renewable-geography diagnostic

- The revised file `market_data_IEEE_9Bus_distributed_congestion_PVshift20_N3_N9.json` produced a converged Jacobi result of only 11.282 MW / 22.564 MWh after 26 iterations, mainly at N2.
- This collapse is economic rather than a generic Jacobi failure. The revision was not a small siting-only perturbation: renewable nameplate fell from about 776 MW to 535 MW, maximum hourly renewable availability fell from about 716 MW to 420 MW, and four negative-system-net-load hours disappeared. Large concentrated N8 PV was replaced by smoother N8 wind and much smaller N3 PV.
- A future geography-only sensitivity should preserve total renewable availability in every hour and change only its nodal allocation.

## Strategic-operation run interrupted

- A two-sided strategic bid-price run was started on the original data under `model/output/strategic_bid_prices_old_data_2026-07-31/seidel_damping025_epsilon1e-6/order_I1-I2-I3-I4/`.
- Settings: damping 0.25, 200 MW nodal limit, 100 maximum sweeps, bid prices enabled, direct bid-price epsilon `1e-6`, no proximal penalty, absolute tolerances 0.5 MW-equivalent for capacity, 0.25 MW for offers, and 0.5 EUR/MWh for prices, required for three consecutive sweeps.
- The current strategic driver is not full Jacobi. It performs one common-snapshot Jacobi initializer and then Gauss--Seidel updates.
- The initializer completed and only Seidel iteration 1 completed before the run was manually interrupted because of runtime. The first sweep had `dStrategy=29.97 MW-eq`, `dOffer=8.34 MW`, and `dPrice=9.57 EUR/MWh`; it was far from convergence. There is no completed strategic equilibrium to report.

## Modelling direction and implementation work

1. Add a real `--update-rule jacobi|seidel` option to `model/epec_strategic_operation_diagonalization.py`.
2. In strategic Jacobi mode, freeze one complete rival snapshot per sweep: MW, MWh, charge/discharge quantity offers, and both bid-price arrays. Solve every investor against that same snapshot, then apply all damped proposals simultaneously and finally apply the shared-limit feasibility safeguard.
3. Preserve deterministic result assembly in configured investor-ID order even when solves finish in another order. Checkpoint only after a complete sweep.
4. Add `--parallel-workers` for Jacobi mode. The four best responses are independent and should run in separate processes, not shared threads, because each constructs and solves its own Pyomo/Ipopt model. Do not share mutable Pyomo models between workers. Serialize market data, configuration, the frozen snapshot, and investor ID; return only response data needed by the parent.
5. Start with up to four workers but benchmark one, two, and four workers because simultaneous Ipopt/MUMPS solves may compete for CPU and memory. Capture per-investor logs, propagate failures/timeouts clearly, and retain a serial Jacobi path for reproducibility.
6. Verify parallel correctness with a one-sweep serial-versus-parallel comparison from the same snapshot. Capacities, offers, prices, objectives, termination states, and strong-duality gaps should match within numerical tolerance.

## Regularization and convergence experiments still needed

- The direct epsilon penalty should remain a small selector on bid prices only. `1e-6` is the established first value, but compare it with zero and nearby magnitudes to confirm that physical investment and withholding are not being materially changed.
- Test unregularized strategic Jacobi first. If it cycles, test a small fixed or staircase proximal penalty on the full strategy. A penalized fixed point is only a numerical/equilibrium-selection candidate and needs a final unregularized best-response audit.
- Convergence should require absolute changes for three or more consecutive sweeps in: capacity MW/MWh norm, effective withholding/quantity offers, and economically relevant bid prices.
- Also export raw undamped best-response residuals. Damped step size alone can understate deviation from a true fixed point.
- Canonicalize prices attached to zero quantities and slack quantity offers before measuring convergence so economically irrelevant variables do not prevent stopping.
- Track aggregate physical capacity/siting separately from ownership, offers, and prices. A physically stable fleet with cycling ownership or bids must not be called a fully converged strategy equilibrium.
- Continue reporting projection events, solver failures, strong-duality gaps, joint-reclear prices/profits, and optimistic-versus-settled profit gaps.

## Documentation change

- `Overleaf/model_extension.tex` now correctly states that capacity-only supports Jacobi and Seidel, while strategic operation currently uses a Jacobi initializer followed by Seidel. It explicitly identifies fully simultaneous strategic Gauss--Jacobi as the preferred modelling direction for avoiding artificial within-sweep early/late-mover advantages, while retaining convergence and equilibrium-selection caveats.

After verification, rerun the original-data two-sided bid-price case with epsilon `1e-6`, damping 0.25, and the existing absolute convergence criteria.
