# Strategic Gauss--Jacobi 200 MW result

## Current objective

Assess the four-investor strategic-operation EPEC under full simultaneous
Gauss--Jacobi updates, including MW/MWh investment, hourly quantity offers, and
two-sided bid prices at a 200 MW nodal limit.

## Implementation and verification

- `model/epec_strategic_operation_diagonalization.py` now supports
  `--update-rule jacobi|seidel` and process-based `--parallel-workers`.
- Jacobi freezes the complete rival strategy for a sweep, solves all investors,
  then applies damped responses simultaneously and projects only if necessary.
- A same-checkpoint serial-versus-four-worker sweep matched all capacities,
  offers, prices, objectives, termination states, strong-duality gaps, and joint
  price bounds exactly. Four workers reduced observed sweep time from roughly
  six to three minutes.

## Run and findings

Output: `model/output/strategic_bid_prices_old_data_2026-07-31/jacobi_damping025_epsilon1e-6_parallel4/order_I1-I2-I3-I4/`.

- Settings: Jacobi, four workers, damping 0.25, 200 MW limit, strategic prices,
  three-sweep stopping rule, and 100 total iterations.
- No proximal penalty, proximal staircase, or dispatch regularization was used.
  The only objective selector was `strategic_epsilon_penalty=1e-6` on squared
  bid prices; final realized penalties were at most EUR 0.0247/day.
- The run stopped at iteration 100 without convergence and never satisfied the
  0.5 EUR/MWh price-step tolerance. At iteration 100 the damped/raw residuals
  were 0.0967/0.3867 MW-equivalent for capacity, 0.4445/1.7781 MW for offers,
  and 9.0203/36.0811 EUR/MWh for prices.
- Final investment was 177.129 MW / 594.405 MWh: 166.492 MW at N8 and
  10.636 MW at N6. I3 owned 56.214 MW, I4 owned 120.915 MW, and I1/I2 were
  effectively zero. This is close to the capacity-only Jacobi fleet.
- Every recorded MPEC solve was optimal, the maximum strong-duality gap was
  about 0.00191, the 200 MW limit stayed slack, and no projection occurred.
  Nonconvergence is therefore a strategy-selection/best-response issue rather
  than a solver or feasibility failure.
- Interpretation: aggregate physical investment and siting are stable, but the
  complete price-and-quantity strategy is not an equilibrium. Do not report the
  iteration-100 state as converged.
- Because the run resumed from iteration 3, exported histories cover iterations
  4--100 even though the final state is iteration 100.
