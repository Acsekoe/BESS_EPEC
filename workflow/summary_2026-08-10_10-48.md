## Mathematical decisions

Both MPECs implement the same fixed-demand capacity game:

- endogenous nodal BESS MW and MWh capacity;
- fixed rival BESS capacities represented as separate ISO-operated units;
- shared nodal MW connection limit;
- fixed-demand DC market clearing with PTDF line constraints;
- generation, BESS charge/discharge, periodic SOC, and degradation cost;
- I1 merchant at 8% WACC;
- I2 merchant at 12% WACC;
- I3 wind-heavy renewable portfolio at 8% WACC;
- I4 solar-heavy renewable portfolio at 8% WACC.

The strong-duality formulation is a nonconvex NLP solved locally with Ipopt.
The KKT formulation is an exact MILP with continuous investment capacity and
binary Big-M complementarity. No capacity discretisation is used.

For the KKT objective:

- total storage spot revenue is replaced by the exact system-payment identity;
- fixed rival operating profits are removed using their storage-capacity
  scarcity rents;
- owned generator rent is linearised exactly as
  `-ownership_share * generation_capacity * nu_gen`.

An optional L1 proximal best-response regularizer is compatible with both
formulations. Its default coefficient is zero.

## Jacobi implementation

Every sweep uses one frozen rival-capacity snapshot. All four investor models
respond to that same snapshot, after which their damped responses are applied
simultaneously.

Convergence is checked using raw best-response deviations in MW and MWh, not
the smaller damped iterate changes. Timed-out or otherwise nonoptimal responses
are recorded but are not applied and cannot trigger convergence.

Parallel investor solves are available through separate worker processes:

```powershell
python model/run_model.py --formulation strong-duality --parallel-workers 4
python model/run_model.py --formulation kkt-bigm --parallel-workers 4
```

`--parallel-workers` accepts 1–4 and defaults to 1. Each worker builds and
solves one MPEC. Parallel KKT workers use one HiGHS thread each. `--tee` is
restricted to one worker to avoid interleaved solver logs.

## Current limitations

- No converged four-investor equilibrium is claimed yet.
- Strong duality with Ipopt is a local nonconvex solve.
- The KKT MILP gives a global formulation, but HiGHS may require substantial
  time to prove optimality. Nonoptimal incumbents are never treated as best
  responses.
- Lower-level prices can remain dual-degenerate; embedded optimistic prices
  should not automatically be interpreted as a unique independent settlement.
