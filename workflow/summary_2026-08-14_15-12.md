# Strategic bidding MPEC handoff

## Current objective

Compare strategic BESS operation under:

1. combined charging/discharging price and quantity bids; and
2. price bids only with full physical MW availability,

using the same Scholtes-relaxed lower-level KKT formulation and moving L1
proximal weight `rho=0.01`.

## Important modeling decisions

- A charging pair is `(maximum charging MW, willingness-to-pay)`; a discharge
  pair is `(maximum discharging MW, sell offer)`.
- The ISO objective contains generation cost plus discharge offer cost minus
  charging willingness-to-pay. Storage settlement remains realized dispatch
  at nodal LMP; bids are not pay-as-bid.
- Physical SOC transition, SOC energy bounds, and daily periodicity belong to
  the lower-level ISO problem. Investment headroom/duration and bid-price
  admissibility belong to the upper level.
- The price-only full-availability model has no quantity variables or
  offer-deliverability constraints. It uses the single nonredundant physical
  inverter constraint `P_charge + P_discharge <= X_power`, so either direction
  can use the complete installed MW without double-using the inverter.
- `offer_discharge >= bid_charge / eta^2` prevents a profitable same-hour
  storage loop. There is no binary charge/discharge exclusivity; tiny
  simultaneous operation may remain under the epsilon relaxation and must be
  audited.
- The moving proximal term is L1, not quadratic. The combined model covers
  capacities, quantities, and scaled prices; the new price-only relaxed-KKT
  model covers capacities and scaled prices. The default price scale is
  `10 EUR/MWh`.

## Implemented formulations

- `strategic-price-quantity`: combined capacity, hourly quantity bids, and
  hourly prices using relaxed KKT.
- `strategic-price-relaxed-kkt`: new price-only, full-availability relaxed-KKT
  formulation.
- The older `strategic-operation` selector remains price-only with full
  availability but uses primal/dual feasibility plus strong duality, not
  relaxed KKT. `--complementarity-epsilon` has no effect on that older path.

## Combined price-quantity 10-sweep result

Analyzed output: `model/output/run_2026-08-14_14-33-04/`.

- Formally converged after recovery from one I3 `maxIterations` response in
  sweep 3; sweeps 4--10 were all optimal and produced seven stable sweeps.
- Final raw residuals: `0.000172 MW` power, `0.000400 MWh` energy,
  `0.003470 MW` quantities, and `0.012106 EUR/MWh` prices.
- Final fleet: `187.183 MW / 547.199 MWh` (`2.923 h`). Each investor owns
  approximately `46.796 MW`.
- Nodal MW totals: N3 `80.984`, N8 `49.944`, N6 `44.085`, N9 `12.170`; other
  nodes are numerically zero.
- Average hourly charging availability was `24.376 MW` (`13.02%` of fleet);
  average discharge availability was `21.357 MW` (`11.41%`). Charging peaked
  at `186.808 MW` in hour 14; discharge peaked at `60.045 MW` in hour 22.
- Quantity-weighted prices were essentially zero. The common final clearing
  produced LMPs of `41.32--60.01 EUR/MWh` and no negative lambdas.
- A same-capacity/same-price full-availability counterfactual changed
  generation cost by only `0.000006 EUR/day` and dispatch by less than
  `0.00002 MWh`. The quantity profiles tightly reproduce unconstrained
  dispatch rather than causing economically material withholding.
- Latest maximum KKT product was `0.001001451` at epsilon `0.001`; maximum
  absolute primal-dual gap was about `2.96 EUR/day`.

## Verification of the new price-only relaxed-KKT model

- A fixed nonzero-price ISO clearing reproduced primal and dual objectives
  exactly; maximum KKT product was below `8e-13`.
- The model contains no `ChargeBidMW` or `DischargeBidMW` variables and no
  separate redundant directional power bounds; it has one shared-inverter
  constraint per unit-node-hour.
- One-investor Ipopt solve terminated optimally at approximately
  `187.182 MW / 547.195 MWh`. Maximum relaxed product was `0.001003643` and the
  primal-dual gap was `0.887 EUR/day`.
- A four-worker first-sweep integration test solved all four best responses
  optimally in about 26 seconds. This is not an equilibrium result.
- Format-v3 price-state checkpoint round-trip, strategic bid CSV export,
  summary generation, Python compilation, and `git diff --check` passed.
- The attempted run under `model/output/run_2026-08-14_14-56-12/` used the old
  strong-duality `strategic-operation` formulation and later encountered
  worker errors. Do not use it for the relaxed-KKT comparison.
