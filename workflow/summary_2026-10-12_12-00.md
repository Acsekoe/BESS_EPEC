### Why the exact I1 MPEC does not close its gap

- **The bound never leaves its root value.** It stays at 2 217.93 for about
  1 million nodes, unchanged with the linear objective and with
  `--dual-m 2e4`. Neither the size of M nor the objective is the cause.
- **Symmetry between twin nodes is real.**
  - N9 alone is proven optimal in 2.6 s (1 896.06).
  - N3 + N9 stays at a 10 % gap. N3 is radial (line L93 only), so up to
    130 MW storage at N3 is exactly equivalent to storage at N9.
- **N3 is not the cause of the four-node gap.** With N6 + N8 + N9 the
  bound is still 2 217.93.
- **Storage dispatch is not unique.**
  - At I1's capacity, 92 of 219 storage values vary by more than 0.1 among
    market solutions within 0.001 EUR of the optimal cost (15 unresolved).
  - At night the state of charge can be anywhere from 0 to full.
  - Cause: morning (hours 1–9) and evening (hours 18–24) both clear at
    60 EUR/MWh, so selling in either block is worth the same.
  - Each such dispatch is a different binary configuration, so the search
    sees many equivalent solutions. This is the main suspect for the stuck
    bound.
- **328 of the 1 640 complementarity pairs are degenerate.** They belong to
  load shedding at six zero-demand nodes and to PV at night, where the
  variable is fixed by `0 <= x <= 0`. Only the difference of the two
  multipliers is determined, which explains the 115 multipliers sitting at M.

### Making N3 differ from N9 (`scripts/diagnostics/n3_lmp_variants.py`)

**Why it is hard.** N3 is radial, so its price differs from N9's only in hours
when L93 binds. Generator G3 at N3 runs only in hour 20 (26.2 MW), so without
storage L93 carries at most 26.2 MW.

**Results with I1 storage of 20 MW / 80 MWh:**

| Variant | N3 vs N9 prices | Storage profit at N3 / N9 (EUR/day) |
|---|---|---|
| Base, or L93 ≥ 26.2 MW | identical | 835.29 / 835.29 |
| L93 < 26.2 MW | N9 goes into scarcity in hour 20 (about 7 800–9 900 EUR/MWh; at 10 MW, 8.3 MWh shed) | – |
| 10 % of N5's load at N3 all day, L93 = 40–45 MW | N3 import-constrained 14–16 h, midday included | storage at N3 earns nothing |
| **10 % of N5's load at N3 in hours 17–22 only, L93 = 40 MW** | **N3 above N9 by 7–15 EUR/MWh in 5 evening hours** | **1 227.68 / 692.29** |

- **The evening-load variant works, with two caveats.**
  - It also changes the pocket: N9's profit falls from 835 to 692.
  - It breaks the indifference only while L93 binds. Once storage at N3
    relieves the evening congestion, further MW at N3 and N9 are again
    equivalent. Whether the best response becomes unique must be checked in
    the MPEC.
- **A battery larger than a binding line earns exactly zero operating
  margin.** Its capacity never binds, so it earns no scarcity rent. Test
  sizes must therefore stay below the line limit.

## Proposed fixes (not implemented)

1. **Fix variables bounded at zero and drop their pairs.** This is an exact
   reformulation and removes the 328 degenerate pairs.
2. **Small convex storage operating cost** `0.5 * delta * (charge^2 + discharge^2)`.
   - Makes dispatch unique, much as eps does for prices.
   - This is a new assumption: ohmic losses and C-rate degradation can justify
     it, but delta is stylized.
   - About 10 EUR/day at delta = 1e-3 for 50 MW over 8 hours.
   - Implemented like the curtailment cost: primal, Dorn dual and
     `profit_linear`.
3. **Evening load at N3 plus an L93 limit**, instead of dropping N3. This
   changes the benchmark data; the load share and line limit are to be decided.

## Warm-start workflow (discussed, not implemented)

1. **Candidates:** zero investment, relaxed results, earlier exact results.
2. **Scoring:** each candidate by its exact unique-price reclear profit.
3. **Building the start:** clear the market at the best candidate, set each
   binary from the binding constraints, then solve the MPEC with capacities
   and binaries fixed (an LP/QP) to get consistent prices and multipliers.
4. **Fallback:** fix only the capacities.
5. **Solving:** pass that point as the MIP start, and save `start_profile.json`.

Open decisions:
- one start or several;
- whether to export the incumbent at the time limit (currently nothing is
  exported);
- the gap target per investor.
