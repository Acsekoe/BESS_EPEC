# Session summary: degradation, SOS1-only MPEC, IEEE 5-bus two-pocket case

Written 2026-10-12 16:19 (Europe/Vienna). Follows `summary_2026-10-12_12-00.md`.
Solvers: Gurobi 13.0.3, Ipopt 3.14.20; Python 3.13, Pyomo 6.10.1.
Evidence tables for the decisions: `complementarity_and_degradation_2026-10-12.md`.

## Decisions

- **Degradation d = 3 EUR/MWh** (was 15). Capex already pays for the whole BESS
  over a fixed 15 years at constant MWh; one cycle/day (about 5 500 cycles) is
  within LFP cycle life. So d prices only the capacity a cycle wears out,
  replaced by augmentation: 62 000 EUR/MWh (BNEF 2025 pack, $70/kWh) x 30 %
  fade / 6 000 cycles = about 3. Sensitivity: d = 0 (NREL ATB convention) and
  d = 10 (cell replacement cost). Cycle counts are datasheet values (uncertain).
- **Dispatch cost slope 1e-4 EUR/MWh per MW**, separate from d:
  `0.5 * slope * (charge^2 + discharge^2)` in primal, Dorn dual, both profit
  forms. A selection device that makes dispatch unique; stylized. It does not
  unstick MPEC bounds.
- **SOS1 is the only complementarity method.** Big-M, bilinear products,
  relaxed products, strong duality, and the Ipopt relaxed MPEC were compared on
  I1/N9 and removed. All share the same root relaxation; SOS1 was fastest and
  needs no multiplier bound. Objective: strong-duality `profit_linear`
  (prices unbounded); settlement `profit` is reported as a check.
- **Quadratic costs stay** (balancing eps, curtailment, dispatch slope).
- **All old outputs deleted** (fresh start).
- **Warm start stays out of `run.py`** until we discuss why investors with
  owned generation are hard to solve.

## Results

IEEE-9 (`capacities_capex120_curtail_d3_slope1e-4.json`, eps 1e-4, I1, rivals zero):

| case | result |
|---|---|
| N9 only, SOS1 | proven optimal in 5 s: 172.73 MW / 450.62 MWh, 6902.13 EUR/day, verified |
| N9, d = 0 / 10 / 15 | 8220 / 3828 / 1894 EUR/day (Big-M runs; capacity 173 / 159 / 109 MW) |
| N3 N6 N8 N9, SOS1 | stopped at 655 s: incumbent 8079.83, bound 8267.50 (2.3 %) |

- The bound stays at its root value for every formulation.
- Diagnosis: in the root relaxation 99.5 % of the complementarity violation is
  the active investor's own inverter pairs (`mu_power`; its rent cancels in
  `profit_linear`, so the multiplier is free).
- Exact reductions do not help the bound. Presolve already removes the 328
  degenerate pairs (1 640 -> 1 312 sets). 164 line pairs that can never bind
  (L14, L72 always; L45, L57 in some hours) give 1.8x faster nodes and a
  slightly better incumbent (8080.31), same bound.

Modified IEEE 5-bus (`ieee5_*.json`, new; eps 1e-4, all five buses, rivals zero):

| investor | result |
|---|---|
| I2 | proven optimal in 53 s: N2 28.62 MW / 57.24 MWh + N5 93.53 / 325.54, 5553.16 EUR/day, verified |
| I1 | no feasible point in 120 s; warm start: incumbent 9352.15 (N2 22.9 / 74.0, N5 118.8 / 380.9), bound 10507 |
| I3, I4 | not rerun on the two-pocket design; on the single-pocket design no feasible point in 600 s / 83 s, warm start gave gaps 5 % / 3 % after 120 s |

- Design: two pockets at different times. PV at N5 at midday (L35 120, L45 100;
  N5 to -12 EUR/MWh), wind at N2 at night (L12 80, L23 60; N2 to -5). Loads
  scaled to peak 400 MW so wind is curtailed without storage. No price above 67.
- The merchants now site at both N2 and N5.
- Warm start (`scripts/diagnostics/active_set_start.py`): KKT point at a fixed
  capacity from the market's active set in about 0.3 s, exact to 0.01 EUR,
  accepted by Gurobi as MIP start. It finds incumbents but does not close gaps.
