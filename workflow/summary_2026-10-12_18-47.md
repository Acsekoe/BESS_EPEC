## Question

Can the 5-bus MPEC be restricted to the two pocket buses (N2 wind, N5 PV)
without losing the best response? That would avoid the multi-node plateau.

## Findings

### 1. In the current 5-bus case the restriction cannot be defended

Exact unique prices without storage (eps 1e-4, curtailment slope 0.1). The
QP was solved with HiGHS active set, and price = b / eps:

| hour | N1 | N2 | N3 | N4 | N5 | cause |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 52 | -5.2 | 67 | 30 | 55 | wind corridor L12, L23 binds |
| 13 | 38 | 41 | 67 | 25 | -10 | PV corridor L35 binds |
| 15 | -2.8 | -2.4 | 1.0 | -4.5 | -9.0 | system-wide surplus: G1 and G3 off |
| 19 | 52 | 51 | 67 | 55 | 63 | thermal sets prices |

- **Hours 14-15 are a system-wide surplus.** Wind and PV cover the whole
  332 MW load, so every bus falls to about 0.
- **N4 is a mixed bus.** L24 (300 MW) never binds, so the wind pocket is
  not sealed off from N4. N4's DC price is a mix of both sides: about
  30 EUR/MWh at night and 25 at midday.
- **A small battery pays at the excluded buses.** A 1 MW price-taker
  battery (best duration 2-8 h, I1 capex 38.38 EUR/MWh per day, d = 3)
  earns per day per MW: N1 +13.5, N3 +53.9, N4 +72.6 (N2 +228.9,
  N5 +141.9). So storage at N1/N3/N4 is profitable at the margin.

### 2. Numerical caution

- **Price accuracy.** With eps 1e-4, an IPM (Clarabel, tol 1e-9) can leave
  prices a few EUR/MWh off, as `model/README.md` warns for the dual. Profit
  checks need an active-set QP (HiGHS) or Ipopt at 1e-12.
- **Unresolved discrepancy.** At the rounded 5-bus I1 incumbent of the
  16:19 summary (N2 22.9/74.0, N5 118.8/380.9), the exact reclear gives
  **8707.88 EUR/day, not 9352.15**. HiGHS and Clarabel agree. The same
  reclear reproduces I2's optimum (5552.85 vs 5553.16 at 2-decimal
  capacities). Either the incumbent sits on a cliff where 0.1 MW of rounding
  matters, or the unverified time-limit value is off. That run exported
  nothing, so this is unresolved.

### 3. Design variants (no-storage market unless noted)

Wind (N2) and PV (N5) keep their names; the other buses carry no renewables.

| variant | surplus hours | prices N1/N3/N4 | 1 MW battery at N1/N3/N4 [EUR/day per MW] |
|---|---|---|---|
| V0 current | 14, 15 | -4.5..67 | +13.5 / +53.9 / +72.6 |
| V1 current, PV 280 MW | 14, 15 | -2.5..67 | +12.3 / +56.8 / +59.9 |
| V2 current, L24 60 MW | 14, 15 | N4 up to 220.6 | N4 +1569.8 (scarcity) |
| V5 radial pockets, thermal 52/67 | none | 52..67 | -60.2 (all three) |
| V6 = V5 with G1 600 MW | none | 52 | -76.8 (all three) |

**V5 data**
- Lines are five of the seven IEEE 5-bus table lines, dropping 1-3 and 2-5.
  Every reactance comes from the table:
  - L12 N1-N2: x 0.06, 140 MW (wind corridor, radial)
  - L13 N1-N3: x 0.18, 300 MW
  - L14 N1-N4: x 0.18, 300 MW
  - L34 N3-N4: x 0.03, 300 MW
  - L45 N4-N5: x 0.24, 120 MW (PV corridor, radial)
- In table numbering this is lines 1-2, 2-3, 2-4, 3-4, 4-5, with table buses
  1 and 2 relabeled N2 and N1.
- Generation: wind 200 MW, PV 300 MW, G1 250 MW at 52, G3 300 MW at 67.
- Peak loads (current values x 1.25): N1 120, N3 180, N4 150, N5 40.
  Shapes are unchanged.
- Without storage, 505 MWh wind and 631 MWh PV are curtailed per day.

Two intermediate radial variants led to V5:
- **V3** kept the original loads, with pocket lines of 120 and 160 MW.
  N1/N3/N4 sat at 52..67 and a battery there lost 68.5 EUR/day per MW. But
  the lines (280 MW) exceed the night load outside the pockets (216 MW), so
  large pocket storage could still push prices there below 52.
- **V4** used loads x 1.25, a 120 MW wind line and the original 250 MW wind.
  That curtailed 1526 MWh of wind per day, far more than storage could ever
  shift.

**Why V5/V6 keep prices outside the pockets bounded**
- The pocket lines carry at most 140 + 120 = 260 MW. That is below the
  minimum load outside the pockets (270 MW), so G1 always runs.
- Prices at N1/N3/N4 therefore stay at or above 52, and at or below 67
  without shedding (thermal 550 MW against at most 490 MW of load).
- Stress test with I1 storage of 300 MW / 1500 MWh at N2 and
  400 MW / 2000 MWh at N5: still no surplus hour, no shedding, and
  N1/N3/N4 stay at 52 in V5 and V6.
- Pockets stay profitable at zero storage: V5 N2 +128, N5 +172; V6 N2 +98,
  N5 +96 EUR/day per MW.

**Thesis argument (V6)**
- The price at N1/N3/N4 is 52 in every hour for any investment, so a
  battery there earns no arbitrage rent and never covers capex.
- Because the pockets are radial, a battery outside them cannot change their
  congestion either.
- The second point is reasoning only. For RES owners (I3/I4) it is untested.

### 4. MPEC behaviour on the variants (SCIP proxy, LP variant, I1, 240 s)

LP variant = eps 0, no curtailment or dispatch slope, as in the 17:56 summary.

| case | N2 | N5 | N2 + N5 | all five buses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| current | 2971.73, 61 s | 6971.84, 1 s | 9369.30, 47 s | 9369.30, bound 9430.66 |
| V5 | 3325.90, 0.2 s | 6143.38, 53 s | 9007.82, bound 10670.76 | 7630.14, bound 10705.45 |
| V6 | 3325.90, 0.1 s | 4304.25, 0.1 s | 7630.14, 0.2 s | **7630.14, optimal 0.2 s** |

Root vs planner on V5 (`root_vs_planner.py`, Clarabel, all buses):
- eps 1e-4: root 12742.04; planner 17043.73.
- LP variant: both 12280.48.
- Both put storage only at N2 and N5.

What the table shows:
- **V5:** the pockets still interact. Both sell into the 67 EUR/MWh evening
  hours, and storage in either pocket shaves those hours to 52. The joint
  two-node MPEC does not close in 240 s; its incumbent (9007.82) is below
  the sum of the single-node optima.
- **V6:** the pockets separate. The N2 + N5 optimum equals the sum of the
  single-node optima. With all five buses open, the MPEC proves the same
  optimum in 0.2 s and builds only at N2 and N5.
- **Caveat on V6:** in the LP variant the V6 merchant optimum equals the
  planner's, because no price falls before curtailment is gone. In the
  working model, market power comes only through the curtailment price
  slope.

## Decisions

None. Recommendation for discussion:
- Rebuild the 5-bus case as V6, with lines outside the pockets set high
  enough never to bind (about 1000 MW).
- Run the full five-bus MPEC instead of restricting nodes.
- Keep V5 as a sensitivity with competition for the evening peak.

## Verification and limitations

- **Solvers.** Prices and profits come from HiGHS active set. Root and
  planner values come from Clarabel. The MPEC runs are SCIP on the LP variant
  (a proxy, not the working eps/slope model in Gurobi).
- **Price-taker check.** The 1 MW battery uses each variant's no-storage
  prices or the stated storage prices. It is a marginal test, not a full
  MPEC with storage at N1/N3/N4.
- **Untested channels.** RES owners (I3/I4) were not tested on V5/V6, and
  neither were rivals' storage or core lines at 1000 MW.
