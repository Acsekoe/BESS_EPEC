## Direction (user)

Exact MPEC best responses inside an equilibrium loop are intractable here:

- the owner proofs do not close (section 4);
- at a step the equilibrium is not unique anyway: whoever fills the pocket gets
  it (allocation, `summary_2026-10-09_13-33.md`).

So we probably go back to Ipopt (local relaxed MPEC) with good warm starts from
the step search (section 5), and use Gurobi only at the end. There, each
investor's MPEC starts from its equilibrium capacity under a time limit; the
bound limits the possible deviation gain (an epsilon-equilibrium statement).
The removed Ipopt MPEC is in git:
`git show 1493b7f:model/mpec_relaxed.py` (deleted in `a039a82`).

## Findings

### 1. 8708 vs 9352 (current case, I1): a needle, not a solver error

- Gurobi pool incumbents are exact KKT points. On six pool solutions, the pool
  objective matched the exact reclear to 0.001 EUR, with complementarity
  products of 1e-5 or less.
- A box MPEC with +-0.06 MW around the rounded 16:19 incumbent is proven in 26 s:
  9353.66 at N2 22.96 / 74.01 and N5 118.80 / 380.88 (exact 9352.90). The rounded
  point (N2 22.9 / 74.0, N5 118.8 / 380.9) gives exactly 8708.04 (HiGHS: 8707.88).
- Exact profit along N2 power (other capacities fixed):

  | N2 MW | 22.95 | 22.96 | 22.97 | 22.98 | 22.99+ |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | profit | 8711.8 | 9352.9 | 9354.2 | 7301.2 | about 6442 |

  All four coordinates have cliffs within 0.01-0.1 MW.
- eps only narrows the needle; it does not create it:

  | eps | needle top (N2 MW) | width |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1e-5 | 22.906-22.960 | 0.055 MW |
  | 1e-4 | 22.960-22.972 | 0.013 MW |
  | 1e-3 | none (maximum along the line 8704.5) | - |

- 9353.66 is only the box optimum. A 20 s warm-started run found 9558.78
  (bound 10 250); it was not exported (the 600 s check was stopped).

### 3. V6 merchants: proven fast, I1 sits on a one-sided edge

| | result | MW / MWh | root | planner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | proven in 1 s, 8042.38 | N2 69.995 / 456.675, N5 98.035 / 478.761 | 8094.93 | 12 396.20 |
| I2 | proven in 83 s, 1264.95 | N2 16.28 / 92.72, N5 51.94 / 227.78 | 1294.66 | 2587.66 |

- Both build only at N2/N5. With I1's optimum installed, N1/N3/N4 stay at 52 in
  every hour.
- `verification_passed` is false only because the thresholds are absolute
  (1e-4 EUR). The reclear cost gap is about 2.4e-3 EUR on a market cost of
  225 734 EUR; the exact profits agree to 1e-3 EUR.
- **I1 is at the step.** N2 energy equals what it can fill:
  487.9 MWh charged x 0.936 = 456.7 MWh. With 0.05 MWh more, its energy limit
  stops binding, the N2 daytime price falls from 52 to 3.1 in 9 of 10 hours,
  and profit drops to -9445. Less energy or more power changes profit by
  5 EUR per MW or less. The planner chooses the same N2 size.
- **I2 is a smooth interior optimum:** +-1 MW changes its profit by 1.2 EUR or less.

### 4. Owners (V6): bounds stay loose

| I3/I4, 600 s | start (exact) | incumbent | bound |
|---|---|---|---|
| I3 cold (`run.py`) | - | 40 180 | 104 627 |
| I3, start = I1's capacity | 53 177.80 | 53 478.55 | 85 883 |
| I3, step start | 53 478.41 | 53 478.55 | 108 213 |
| I4, step start | 66 194.51 | 66 194.88 (exact 66 192.86) | 68 312 (3.2 %) |

- I3's root relaxation (129 943) already chooses the best start's capacity
  (N2 69.99 / 456.68, N5 136.35 / 575.56). The gap is in how the relaxation
  values the owner's generation, not in where it builds.
- **Warm-start bug, fixed.** Gurobi rejected the I3 start ("User MIP start did
  not produce a new incumbent"):
  - a zero-member threshold of 1e-5 misclassified small nonzero quantities at
    the knife-edge capacity, so the fixed-active-set QP was infeasible;
  - barrier still returned a point, with a 2.05e-6 violation.

  Threshold 1e-7 and dual simplex fix it: residual 1e-12, start accepted.

### 5. Step search (market only, no Gurobi)

`step_candidates.py` works in three stages:

1. reads the step energies off the no-storage market;
2. evaluates single-node candidates around each step, then their combinations;
3. refines the best one. Each MW move re-climbs the MWh (needed on I1's ridge,
   where single-coordinate moves stalled at 7962).

| case | step search | reference |
|---|---|---|
| V6 I1 | 8042.35 (91 s) | proven 8042.38 |
| V6 I2 | 1264.95 (60 s) | proven 1264.95 |
| V6 I3 | 53 478.41 (100 s) | Gurobi 600 s: 53 478.55 |
| V6 I4 | 66 194.51 (93 s) | Gurobi 600 s from it: 66 194.88 |
| V5 I1 | 10 693.85 | Gurobi from it, stopped at 110 s: 11 076.94, bound 11 355 (not exported) |
| V5 I2 / I3 / I4 | 4 949.14 / 55 169.05 / 66 196.46 | - |
| current I1 | 8 840.05 (1051 s) | 9353.66 (box), 9558.78 (Gurobi) |

- Run times without a value were 6-17 min because Gurobi shared the CPU.
- Ipopt failed once each on V5 I1 and I3; those candidates were skipped.
- **Reliability.** The step search is exact on the V6 merchants. It falls short
  where steps interact: V5 by about 3.5 %, and the current case misses the needle.

### 6. Solver settings checked

- **`NonConvex` has no effect on the MPEC.** V6 I1 runs identically with 2, 0,
  and -1 (2022 nodes, 1.162 work units). The MPEC is convex apart from the
  SOS1 sets; only `ranges` (`extrema`) needs `NonConvex=2`. The nonconvex
  formulations tried before lost (10-08 bilinear objective; 10-12 products,
  see `complementarity_and_degradation_2026-10-12.md`).
- **Bounding the duals does not help.** With every price and multiplier bounded
  at +-2e4 or +-1.1e4, V6 I3's root stays at 129 942.6 and the 60 s bound is no
  better. The owned `mu_gen_upper` enters `profit_linear` with
  -(1 - share) * capacity, so the bound cannot run away.
- **Threads.** Gurobi already uses all 16 threads. More threads would not close
  a bound that is weak in the relaxation itself.
