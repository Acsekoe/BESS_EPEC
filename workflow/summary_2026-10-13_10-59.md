# Session summary: relaxed MPEC restored; strategic storage bids

Written 2026-10-13 10:59 (Europe/Vienna). Follows `summary_2026-10-12_20-53.md`.
Base commit `5534d1f`.

Machine: AMD Ryzen AI 7 445 (6 cores, 12 threads), Windows 11. Python 3.13,
Pyomo 6.10.1, Gurobi 13.0.3 (12 threads), Ipopt 3.14.20.

All runs: V6 5-bus case (`ieee5_v6_*.json`), eps = 1e-4, all five buses,
rivals at zero, profits in EUR/day. "Exact" means the unique-price reclear and
settlement at the chosen capacity (and bids).

## Decisions

- **Relaxed MPEC is back** as `model/mpec_relaxed.py`: the same blocks as
  `mpec.py`, with Scholtes products `slack * mu <= epsilon` and Ipopt continuation
  (epsilon 1 to 1e-6). It is a fast local method; Gurobi SOS1 stays the proof.
- **Strategic bids are an option** (`--strategic-bids`, both MPECs). Truthful
  bids are the default, so all earlier results are unchanged.
  - Each storage has an hourly charge bid (price to buy, truthful -0.5 d) and a
    discharge offer (price to sell, truthful 0.5 d) per node.
  - The active investor chooses its bids in [-VOLL, VOLL] with
    eta^2 * offer >= bid (no negative-cost same-hour loop). Rival bids are fixed
    profile inputs (`charge_bid_eur_per_mwh`, `discharge_offer_eur_per_mwh`).
  - Bids need `--balancing-eps > 0` (unique prices for the exact reclear).
- **Objective stays `profit_linear`** (strong duality). It counts the active
  storage at its true degradation cost instead of its bids, so no bid * quantity
  product appears (Ruiz and Conejo 2009). No return to the bilinear objective.
- **Settlement is pay-as-clear.** Bids enter only the ISO's objective (as-bid
  cost) and the charge/discharge stationarity. Everyone is paid the LMP (the
  multiplier of the nodal balance); the investor's profit subtracts its true
  costs. A bid moves the LMP through the dispatch, and sets it when the storage
  is the marginal unit at its node.

- Local, not a proof: I2 from the step start stopped 0.24 EUR lower.
- Toy (`toy_market.json`): exact optimum 140 at 5 MW from starts of 1, 3 and 8 MW.

### 2. With bids Gurobi proves every V6 investor within seconds

| investor | capacity only | bids, relaxed (50 MW / step start) | bids, Gurobi SOS1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | 8042.38 | 8094.93 / 8094.93 | 8094.93, proven in 1 s |
| I2 | 1264.95 | 1294.66 / 1294.66 | 1294.66, proven in 1 s |
| I3 | 53 478.54 | 124 420.44 / 124 554.76 | 124 554.73, proven in 3 s |
| I4 | 66 194.71 | 75 431.30 / 75 431.30 | 75 431.43, proven in 1 s |

- **Why Gurobi is fast:** the root bound did not improve; the optimum moved
  up to it.
  - Merchants: optimum = the capacity MPEC's root relaxation (8094.93 and
    1294.66 in the 20:53 summary). Bids free the investor's own storage
    conditions, which that relaxation drops. I1: 855 nodes.
  - I3: root 129 943 in both models; the gap fell from 143 % to 4 % and closed
    in 5 157 nodes.
- **I3 mechanism (portfolio market power through charging):**
  - its N2 battery charges about 1 MW more per night hour, just enough to absorb
    the curtailed wind;
  - its charge bid is then the marginal demand in the wind pocket and sets the
    night N2 price at 52 instead of 0;
  - its 80 % wind share earns that price; the storage's own arbitrage loses a
    little. This is an optimistic single-investor result.
- **Bids are not unique.** Any bid beyond the price range supports the same
  dispatch (I1 offers 6 729 at N2 at night = "never discharge here"). Read the
  dispatch and recleared prices as the result, not the bid levels.

### 3. Bids remove the cliff at the step (I1)

Capacities fixed at the capacity-only optimum (N2 70.0 / 456.675, N5 98.0 / 478.8),
only N2 energy varied (N2 power raised to keep the 8 h maximum duration):

| N2 energy | truthful bids (exact reclear) | strategic bids (SOS1, fixed capacity) |
|---|---|---|
| -1.00 MWh | 8 038.72 | 8 068.49 |
| 0 | 8 042.37 | 8 070.89 |
| +0.05 MWh | -9 983.34 | 8 068.97 |
| +0.50 MWh | -10 000.61 | 8 051.70 |
| +10 MWh | -10 365.26 | 7 687.06 |

- With bids the investor does not offer the extra MWh, so the price holds.
  Profit falls by about 38 EUR per extra MWh: the daily capex of 1 MWh
  (120 000 x CRF(8 %, 15 y) / 365.25).
- Smoothness itself does not speed up Gurobi (it branches on SOS1 sets). Speed
  and smoothness share one cause: bids remove the price-taker dispatch of the
  investor's own storage, which produced both the cliffs and the gap to the root.
- Scratch diagnostic, given by its specification: for each energy offset,
  `unique_price_reclear` + `settlement_profit` (truthful), and
  `build_mpec(..., strategic_bids=True)` with `X_power`/`X_energy` fixed, solved
  by Gurobi (strategic).

### 4. Verification notes

- Relaxed runs check the **signed** largest product against epsilon. Negative
  products (down to -5e-5) are a 1e-8 primal tolerance times multipliers up to
  9e4. Ipopt now projects onto the original bounds (`honor_original_bounds`).
- SOS1 bid runs for I2 and I4 report `verification_passed: false` only through
  the absolute 1e-4 EUR thresholds: profit identity and primal-dual gap 5e-4 EUR
  on an as-bid market cost of about -4e5 EUR (the ISO objective, not money paid).

## Verification and limitations

- 14 unit tests pass (`python -m unittest discover -s scripts/tests -v`).
- Every relaxed result is a local optimum from one start; the four-hour bid test
  shows a start-dependent local optimum (300 vs 340).
- Tested on V6 only. Bids are untested on IEEE-9 (four-node plateau), V5 and
  the current 5-bus case.
- With bids several capacity-limited storage investors compete on price
  (Bertrand-Edgeworth-like): a pure equilibrium may not exist.
- Including bids reopens the strategic-operation scope that the capacity-only
  restart (2026-10-07) excluded; the thesis needs a justification.
- Outputs are in `model/output/` (not tracked): `relaxed_v6_*`,
  `relaxed_bids_v6_*`, `sos1_bids_v6_*`.
