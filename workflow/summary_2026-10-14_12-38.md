## Decisions

- **Final market data: the 5-bus test system** (`model/input/five_bus_*.json`,
  the default of `run.py`, built by `scripts/data/build_five_bus.py`). All
  other inputs were removed from `model/input/`; they are at commit `82690b2`.
- **One thermal unit with a rising marginal cost.** G1 600 MW at N1 with
  marginal cost 41 + 0.075 * g EUR/MWh; G3 removed. It stands for a thermal
  merit order. It replaced V7 (G1 300 MW at 52, G3 300 MW at 67), which was the
  test system earlier today.
  - **Why:** in V7 the G3 block was a second price edge at the hub, besides the
    pocket edges. I2's best response sat on it (evening price 66.99), held
    there only by the balancing eps.
- **Pricing stays nodal.** Zonal pricing was discussed and not pursued.
- **Workflow: Ipopt for candidates, Gurobi to check.**
  - Ipopt (`mpec_relaxed`) from several starts finds candidates in seconds.
  - Gurobi (`mpec --start-profile`) starts from the best one and improves,
    proves, or refutes it.
  - `verify` checks equilibria with a Gurobi Cutoff.
  - `--mip-gap 0.01` while exploring.
- **`--nodes` gives only a restricted proof.** Dropping nodes where Ipopt never
  invests is allowed, but the result then only rules out deviations at the
  listed nodes. `verify` records the nodes in `deviation_nodes`.

## Findings

**Market without storage (new system).**
- The hub (N1/N3/N4) follows residual load: 52.06 EUR/MWh at hour 16 (G1 at
  147.5 MW) up to 66.65 at hour 20 (G1 at 342 MW).
- N2 falls to -6.0 at night, with 505 MWh of wind curtailed.
- N5 falls to -14.7 at midday, with 632 MWh of PV curtailed.
- Market cost 260 857 EUR/day.
- Each pocket keeps one price edge (its line congested or not); the hub has
  none.

**Best responses on the new system** (`model/output/five_bus_best_responses/`):

- **The switch made the merchants easy.**
  - I1 is proven within 1 % in 11 s. On V7 the gap was still 7.3 % after
    180 s.
  - I1's N2 battery is now interior (52.5 MW) instead of at the pocket edge
    (V7: 70 MW).
  - I2 no longer depends on the start point. On V7 it ended at 1265 or 2238
    depending on the start.
- **Owners still have several local optima.** They sit where a battery takes a
  whole pocket surplus: N2 35 MW is the hour-22 wind surplus over L12, N5
  146.8 MW the hour-14 PV surplus over L45. I3's Gurobi bound stays loose.
- **V7 comparison (Gurobi from the Ipopt start, 180 s).**
  - The start gives Gurobi an incumbent at once (I3 had none cold after 600 s)
    but does not move the bound.
  - Restricting I1 to fewer nodes did not tighten the bound: 8629 at all
    nodes, 8671 at N1/N2/N5, 8844 at N2/N5.
  - Gurobi improved no Ipopt point on V7.
- **Ipopt bound relaxation fixed.**
  - Ipopt's default `bound_relax_factor` 1e-8 left constraint errors of about
    2e-6.
  - It also put the exact reclear's market cost 2.6e-3 EUR below Gurobi's
    certified value, enough to fail the check of `mpec` runs.
  - With it set to 0 in `solve_nlp`, the reclear matches Gurobi to 6e-7 EUR,
    and all 12 Ipopt runs pass `verification_passed` (violations about 1e-12).

## Limitations

- **Owner results are not proven best responses.** I3 and I4 are local optima.
  Gurobi found a better I3 point (+59 EUR/day), but a time-limited run does not
  export its capacities.
- **`ranges` mostly fails on the new system.** The quadratic cost turns the
  optimal face into a quadratic constraint, and Gurobi stops with SUBOPTIMAL:
  121 of 124 ranges unresolved (V7: 22). No wrong values are reported. The hub
  prices are unique anyway while G1 is between its bounds.
- **No equilibrium check yet.** `verify` has not been run on the new system.
- **The cost slope is not sourced.** 41 + 0.075 * g is calibrated to span the
  former 52-67 range; it is not taken from data.
- **V7 results were not rerun** after the Ipopt fix. Their profits change by
  less than 1 EUR/day.
